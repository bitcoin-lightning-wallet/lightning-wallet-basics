## What this wallet is good at

A desktop wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux that holds your keys, manages channels for you, and keeps
the whole experience to an amount and a confirm button. It is honest about its limits: no card, no
exchange, no mobile build in the current release, no hardware integration.

For someone who wants a self-custodial Lightning balance on a computer and a phrase they control, it is
a direct fit.

## Where another wallet wins

**On a phone.** The current release is desktop. A mobile-first user should pick a mobile wallet rather
than a desktop app with remote access.

**For a custodial convenience balance.** If you want password recovery and no backup discipline, a
custodial app is the honest choice, and pretending otherwise leads to lost phrases.

**For a savings balance.** A hardware wallet on-chain is the standard answer, with the Lightning
balance kept small.

**For running infrastructure.** Node software exists for people who want peer management and routing
income. This is not that.

## How to compare the field

Ignore feature counts and compare custody, recovery and cost. The checklist in
[the best Lightning wallet](https://github.com/bitcoin-lightning-wallet/lightning-wallet-basics/blob/HEAD/best-lightning-wallet-checklist.md)
is the instrument; a comparison table without those columns is marketing.

## The category split

Most wallets fall into four groups: exchanges and payment apps (custodial, convenient), multi-asset
consumer wallets (mixed support, often custodial for Lightning), bitcoin-first wallets (usually
non-custodial), and node software (powerful, demanding).

This wallet is in the third group. Knowing which group a competitor is in tells you more than any
feature list.

## The honest summary

If you want a desktop Lightning wallet whose keys you hold and whose channels you do not have to
manage, this is built for you. If you want a card, a phone app or a trading desk, it is not, and no
comparison page should pretend otherwise.

## Questions people ask

### Is this the best Lightning wallet?

For its specific job, it is a strong fit. "Best" without a threat model is not a question with an
answer.

### Can I use it alongside another wallet?

Yes. A custodial app for convenience and this for self-custody is a common split.

### What is missing that competitors have?

Mobile, a card and hardware integration. Each is a roadmap item rather than a current feature.
